Water Security

Clean Water for the Southern District

"Everyone deserves access to clean and safe water and a healthy environment. For the first time, our daughters can study in the evening instead of spending hours carrying heavy water jerrycans." — Amara K., Community Council President

In the arid regions of the Southern District, access to potable water was a severe issue forcing children to walk up to 10 kilometers daily. IWEREF installed 12 gravity-fed solar-powered water filtration grids. Today, over 3,000 residents enjoy clean water at their doorsteps, boosting public health and school enrollment rates by over 44%.

Location: Southern District Watershed • Completed 2025
Child Health & Education

Make a Child Smile: Health in Schools

"A smile on a child's face is a reminder that joy is contagious, and love is the simplest and most powerful way to spread it." — IWEREF Field Outreach Initiative

By installing solar-powered clean water filtration taps and modern handwashing points across 24 regional schools, IWEREF eliminated seasonal typhoid outbreaks. Children now attend classes consistently, thriving in an environment nourished by safe drinking water and communal support.

Location: 24 Primary Schools • Ongoing Program
Watershed Restoration

Reclaiming the Riverbanks with Native Trees

"The topsoil was eroding away every dry season. Now the root networks of 15,000 indigenous trees hold the valley together." — Joseph M., Cooperative Farming Lead

Severe land erosion was depleting crop yields in rural watersheds. Partnering with local schools and agricultural clusters, IWEREF launched a community-driven afforestation grid. In 18 months, over 15,000 native seedlings were planted, reclaiming critical soil layers and bringing microclimate cooling to the entire river valley.

Location: Highland River Catchment • Ongoing Monitoring
Youth Ambassadors

Next-Gen Water Quality Stewards

"Our students test school tap water weekly and send logs to the district water board. They are proud scientists." — Mr. A. Tariq, Headteacher

By establishing student-run ecology clubs, IWEREF provided test kits to monitor pH, coliform bacteria, and turbidity. Over 600 students have graduated from our eco-stewardship curriculum, serving as lifelong guardians of their local waterways.

Location: 24 Community Schools • Active Program
