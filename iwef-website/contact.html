COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common inquiries regarding our programs, governance, and donation processes.

How does IWEREF select communities for new water installations? ▾

We work in collaboration with regional health departments, indigenous council bodies, and satellite drought indices to identify areas experiencing severe groundwater depletion, child morbidity from contaminated sources, and high walking distance burdens. Every project undergoes a multi-factor hydrological and demographic audit before funding is released.

What proportion of public donations goes directly to field projects? ▾

IWEREF operates with strict financial efficiency: 85% of all unrestricted funds are allocated directly to solar boreholes, pipes, filtration membranes, and tree planting drives. 10% is dedicated to community steward training, and only 5% is allocated to administration and auditing.

Can international volunteers participate in on-the-ground deployments? ▾

Yes. We host structured volunteer cohorts each quarter for hydrological engineers, environmental educators, and project administrators. All field volunteers receive health certifications and cross-cultural training prior to field deployment.

Are donations to IWEREF tax-deductible? ▾

Yes, IWEREF is an officially registered non-profit organization. Donors will automatically receive an official tax-deductible receipt via email immediately after completing their donation.
