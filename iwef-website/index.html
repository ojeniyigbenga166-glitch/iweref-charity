ABOUT US

Integrated Water and Environmental Resources Foundation

We are an organization dedicated to the protection of our most valuable resources. Our mission spans from providing clean water to fighting environmental degradation globally.

With community-driven efforts, we tackle water scarcity, build infrastructure, and empower people to take charge of their sustainable futures.

COMMUNITY TESTIMONIALS & RESULTS

Inspiring Stories Of Environmental Transformation

Water

Clean Water Initiative

In the arid regions of the Southern District, access to potable water was a severe issue forcing children to walk miles daily. IWEREF installed 12 gravity-fed solar-powered water filtration grids. Today, over 3,000 residents enjoy clean water at their doorsteps, boosting public health and school enrollment rates.

School Health

Youth Water Ambassadors

Clean water transforms childhoods. By pairing community boreholes with hygiene education and student eco-clubs in regional schools, we restore health and joy to rural youth, giving them the freedom to learn and smile without the threat of waterborne disease.
