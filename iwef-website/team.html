IWEREF is guided by a dedicated Governing Council and field specialists committed to clean water, ecological restoration, and transparent community impact.

GOVERNANCE & LEADERSHIP

The People Behind IWEREF

Experienced scientists, hydrological engineers, conservationists, and community leaders working together to turn environmental challenges into lasting local solutions.

Dr. Tomi Adeleke

Founder & Executive Director

Environmental scientist with 18+ years leading watershed restoration projects across Africa and Asia, guiding IWEREF’s strategic direction.

Eng. Elena Rostova

Chief Hydrological Engineer

Specialist in solar borehole designs, clean water filtration technologies, and renewable off-grid infrastructure for rural settlements.

Marcus Vance

Conservation Director

Leads native tree planting programs, soil recovery, and ecological corridor restorations alongside local farmers and youth volunteers.

Sarah Al-Mansoor

Community Outreach Lead

Coordinates volunteer onboarding, school hygiene & ecological workshops, and local water governance committees across partner communities.
