OUR STORY

A Decade of Dedication to Humanity

IWEREF (Iwere Foundation) was born out of a simple observation: talent is universal, but opportunity is not. Founded in 2014, the foundation began with a single mobile library serving three rural villages.

Today, we have grown into a multi-disciplinary NGO operating across Nigeria, focusing on the intersection of health, education, and economic stability. Our approach is "Community-Led, Technology-Powered" � we co-create solutions with local leaders, using modern data and tools to ensure every dollar donated has a measurable impact.

OUR FOCUS AREAS

Join Us in Our Mission

Whether you donate your time, expertise, or funds, you are helping build a more equitable world � one community at a time.

Water Scarcity

Providing sustainable access to clean drinking water in drought-prone regions through deep boreholes and solar filtration grids.

Environmental Degradation

Restoring damaged riverbanks, stopping soil erosion, and replanting indigenous tree species in critical ecological buffers.

Climate Resilience

Implementing adaptive rainwater harvesting and flood management infrastructure to protect against extreme weather events.

Health & Sanitation

Constructing hygienic sanitation facilities in rural schools and clinics, breaking the chain of water-borne diseases.

Infrastructure Deficits

Engineering resilient, low-maintenance piping networks and gravity-assisted distribution points for off-grid settlements.

Policy & Advocacy

Advising local councils and national bodies on fair water rights, wetland conservation zoning, and pollution penalties.
