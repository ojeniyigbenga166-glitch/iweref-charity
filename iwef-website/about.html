OUR STORY

A Decade of Dedication to Humanity

IWEREF (Iwere Foundation) was born out of a simple observation: talent is universal, but opportunity is not. Founded in 2014 by Dr. Sarah Iwere, the foundation began with a single mobile library serving three rural villages.

Today, we have grown into a multi-disciplinary NGO operating across Nigeria, focusing on the intersection of health, education, and economic stability. We believe that by addressing these three pillars simultaneously, we can break the cycle of generational poverty.

Our approach is “Community-Led, Technology-Powered.” We don’t just bring solutions; we co-create them with local leaders, using modern data and tools to ensure every dollar donated has a measurable impact.

OUR MISSION & VISION

What We Stand For

Two pillars that define every decision, every program, and every partnership we undertake.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through sustainable access to quality education, transformative healthcare, and economic opportunity — fostering a world where every individual can reach their full potential.

Our Vision

A global landscape where social equity is the standard, and communities are self-reliant, resilient, and thriving in health and knowledge — no one left behind.
